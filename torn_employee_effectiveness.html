Combined stat efficiency
—
Enter stats to calculate.

Formula applied to each required stat:
FLOOR(MIN(45, (45 / required) × stat)) + MAX(0, 5 × LOG₂(stat / required))

Meeting each required stat gives 45 points (90 combined). Every doubling above a requirement adds 5 bonus points for that stat. This calculator uses the requirements in the supplied CSV and does not include other role-specific bonuses.
